## 2. How the runner drives the frozen search concurrently (design choice, not in src/)
The driver (`run_search` / `run_control`) is called unchanged and replayed until it completes. Its injected solvers read
one result file per solve; a missing file raises `Pending` with the jobs needed, and the scheduler runs jobs in
subprocesses as long as they fit in 0.85 x RAM by their estimates and in the vCPU count (EM, band, thermal, FV and
control solves of every reference mixed). A stage mirror submits what the driver will certainly need next (reference,
twin and D bands at t = 0; calibration then the twin/reconstruction thermal solves; the 16 + 16 shortlist L_sub as soon
as each screen ends; the first thermal batch and the inductance-best band as soon as the shortlist's L_sub order exists;
whole thermal batches and the whole near-tie union when the driver asks for one member). The driver alone decides
which results are used. Screens (main and control, every reference) start first, each a 1-thread subprocess that runs
the driver's own `_screen` and records (fingerprint, family) per chunk; replays stream stubs, so the 20,000 candidates
are generated once. Every subprocess gets OPENBLAS/OMP/MKL_NUM_THREADS = MF_L2_FFT_WORKERS = 2 (from
`research/l2_thread_calib/summary.txt`: two-thread mean 5,751 s vs pinned 6,364 s) and MF_L2_THREADS = 1, recorded in
each result. Fail fast: W and D of every reference are built (L1 only) before the first l2_layout solve.

`--selftest` runs this whole path (screens through the real driver, replays, scheduler, row assembly, checks,
metrics) on fake layouts and fake solvers in three planted worlds: headroom (h_lo = 0.21055, equal to a hand
recomputation from the rows to 1e-12), twin-best (h_lo = 0 by the twin rule; the planted F1(b) failure must be caught)
and levelled (L1 floor reached within the first n_first, so K1 is finite: 0.21401, equal to a hand recomputation of
levelling, twin rule and near ties to 1e-12). In every world and for both namespaces the replayed, scheduled driver must
return exactly what a direct single-process `run_search` / `run_control` call returns on the same fake solvers
(inductance_best, a1_best, null_best, near_ties, w_ind, w_ind_recon, w_ctrl, w_a1, thermal_checked, null_new_checks,
levelling). mfos.metrics and research/o003/headroom_metrics.py agree; the declared null gives h_lo <= 0.

Checkpoint identity (attempt 4 fix): every result file name carries a hash of the job's physics inputs (level, rule,
power, h, T_c, frequency), so a changed rule or operating point can never reuse a stale result. Before this fix a
thermal result was keyed by fingerprint and level only.

   thermal job from its own grid (`thermal_fem.estimate_dofs`), and `--plan` uses the measured counts.
2. **The earlier local smoke thermal rule did not fit the Mac.** Doubling h0 and dz_max kept the 0.1 mm edge grading;
   its level 2 on gen2_0 is about 5 M DOFs (~30 GB), estimated at 1.9 GB. That job was running when attempt 1 stopped;

3. **F6(e) does not fit the 80 GB VM.** At Case 4 size the FV level 3 grid has 235 M cells, ~350 GB at E009's

   reference or a decision.
4. 2D_Case_4's stack names its 1 um solder placeholder `none (M5)`, which has no k(T) law; the dev path substitutes
   SAC305 (same k; ~0.5 % of the AlN layer's thermal resistance). Recorded in the spec's dev block.
5. From attempt 1 (unchanged facts): Case 4's twin raises TwinError under raw `measure_rules` (dev overrides in the

## 6. Attempt 5 (2026-11-10): what changed and why
Facts:
- **Case 4's twin gates were not run locally.** They need Case 4's 0.5 mm thk_f band solve, which P3 measured at 8.1 GB on
  this Mac (`research/notes/o006-p3-em-convergence.md`, table line 130). The agent budget is 5 GB, and the exception for one
  process up to 10 GB requires swap < 4 GB. The sandbox denies `sysctl vm.swapusage` (also the numeric MIB through
  ctypes), and `/System/Volumes/VM` holds 4 x 1 GB of allocated swap files, so "under 4 GB" could not be shown. The gates stay
