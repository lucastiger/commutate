## 3. Competing explanations and their predictions

d1 = L1 - L0 and d2 = L2 - L1 of each die's Tj max. "Monotone" = d1 and d2 of one sign and |rho| < 1 (finite GCI).

| | mechanism | prediction V0 | V1 (attach 50 um) | V2 (no 1 um layer) | V3 (V1+V2) | V4 (2R recipe = V1) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **H1** 0 um die attach | with no attach the die sits on top Cu; the missing resistive layer leaves the in-plane spreading error dominant | non-monotone | **monotone**, d1 > 0 | non-monotone, as V0 | monotone | as V1 |
| **H2** 1 um solder layer | 1-cell 1 um layer, lateral/thickness aspect up to 5000, degrades the discrete solution or the solver | non-monotone | non-monotone | **monotone**, d1 > 0 | monotone | as V1 (has the layer) |
| **H3** in-plane geometry / edge grading | the hot spot is the die centre, the corner of the die's four largest cells (2.75 x 2.875 mm interior with 2 x 2 cells at L0); the coarse centre over-predicts the peak (d1 < 0) and at L2 the max moves to a node that did not exist before (d2 > 0) | non-monotone | non-monotone, d1 < 0 | non-monotone, d1 < 0 | non-monotone | non-monotone | 
| **H4** pre-asymptotic levels at Case 4's size, any stack | two error terms of opposite sign (local peak over-prediction decaying fast, global under-prediction of spreading decaying slowly) cross between L1 and L2 | non-monotone | d1 sign may change with the stack, sequence stays non-monotone | as V0 | as V1 | as V1 |

Common to H3 and H4 [I]: the stack variants change the vertical path, not the in-plane grid, so d1 < 0 in every variant. They
separate on the fixed die-centre node: under H3 (hot-spot migration) the die-centre sequence is monotone or has |rho| < 1 and
only the max jumps at L2; under H4 the die-centre sequence itself turns at L2. A further check common to all four: for constant k
on nested conforming Galerkin spaces the compliance, here the power-weighted mean die-top rise, cannot decrease with refinement
(a(u_h, u_h) = l(u_h) and Galerkin orthogonality, Brenner & Scott, *The Mathematical Theory of Finite Element Methods*, 3rd ed.,
2008, ch. 2). A falling area mean would point to the solver (implementation), not to any of H1-H4.

**Results against the predictions** [I]:
- **H2 refuted** as a cause: removing the 1 um layer changes d1 by <= 3e-4 K (V2 vs V0, V3 vs V1) and Tj by 0.012 K; it halves
  the CG iterations, so it matters for cost, not for the sequence. What would revive it: a V2/V3 d2 at L2 that differs from V0/V1's
  by more than ~0.01 K.
- **H1 refuted as the cause of the first (negative) step**: the 50 um die attach shrinks |d1| by 25-31 % but every die keeps
  d1 < 0. Whether the attach changes the second step, and so whether |rho| falls below 1, needs level 2 (VM). With |d1| smaller
  (0.059-0.080 K), V1 needs |d2| < |d1| for a finite GCI; if d2 stays near V0's +0.15-0.17 K, |rho| rises to ~1.9-2.9 and the GCI
  stays +inf.
- **Consistent with H3/H4**: d1 < 0 in every variant, the hottest node is the die centre at L0 and L1 in every variant, and the
  area mean rises by ~0.5 K at the same step. The die-top profile flattens with refinement: the coarse centre over-predicts the
  peak by more than the global under-prediction, while the mean behaves as Galerkin requires (sanity: no implementation signature).
- H3 vs H4 is not decided by levels 0-1: it needs the die-centre node and the area mean at L2 (recorded by the VM run).

## 5. Conclusion (provisional)

**Class: experimental** (the frozen rule's 3-level estimate cannot bound Case 4's Tj max: the sequence is pre-asymptotic at the
die-centre peak), not implementation (V0 reproduces to 1e-11 K; Picard, CG and energy balance are clean; the area mean rises as
Galerkin requires), not model. **Confidence 0.6**: the first step is settled locally on all five variants; the second step and
the H3/H4 split rest on level 2, which only the VM can solve (Case 4 L2 ~9.2 GB > the 5 GB local limit).

Cause, as far as tested [I]: neither the 0 um die attach (H1) nor the 1 um layer (H2) creates the negative first step; it is
in-plane and sits at the die centre, the corner node of the die's four largest cells, which the frozen edge grading leaves at
2.75 x 2.875 mm on a 4 x 6 mm die. gen2_0's hot spots are not die centres and its steps are positive [F], which fits that.
**[S]** The step sizes (|d1| 0.06-0.11 K, d2 0.15-0.17 K) are small against the 2 K limit: the problem is the +inf branch of
gci_capped, not a large error. That is a fact about the metric's branch, not a recommendation.
